About

Welcome to my academic website. I am a mathematics educator and researcher interested in differential geometry and the connections between abstract mathematical ideas and their applications.

This website brings together information about my research, publications, teaching activities, and mathematical resources.

Research interests

My research interests include the following areas.

Differential Geometry

Geometric structures, Riemannian geometry, and complex manifolds.

Complex Geometry

Locally conformally Kähler geometry and Hermitian structures.

Mathematical Analysis

Analytical methods and their connections with geometric problems.

Applications of Mathematics

Mathematical modelling, computational methods, and problem-solving.

Teaching Mathematics in Morocco

Educational resources in mathematics for Moroccan high-school students, CPGE students, and university learners. Find course notes, exercises, corrected problems, and examination preparation materials.

CPGE Mathematics

  • MP and PSI

University Mathematics

Mathematical resources for Moroccan university students, particularly in Licence and Master programmes.

Agrégation in Mathematics

Resources and preparation materials for candidates pursuing the Moroccan and French Agrégation in Mathematics. This section aims to support rigorous mathematical training, problem-solving, and preparation for written and oral examinations.

Moroccan Agrégation

Resources for the Moroccan competitive examination in mathematics, including course notes, exercises, problems, and preparation for oral examinations.

Topics: Algebra, Analysis, Topology, Probability, Numerical Analysis, and Mathematical Modelling.

French Agrégation

Mathematical resources for candidates preparing for the French Agrégation, with an emphasis on theoretical foundations, rigorous proofs, and examination methodology.

Topics: Algebra, Analysis, Geometry, Probability, and Oral Examination Preparation.

Coming soon: downloadable notes, solved exercises, selected problems, and oral examination resources.
